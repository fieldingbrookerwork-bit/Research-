# AI-Subagent Income Streams — Research

Research question: which monthly income streams can run almost entirely on AI sub-agents (prospecting, research, production, outreach) at $0 spend (free tiers only), where the only human step is the founder collecting payment?

**→ Read the full report: [`report.md`](report.md)**

**Verdict (2026-08-26):** Bid Scout — a curated federal-contract intelligence subscription ($49–$79/mo per firm) for one niche of small government contractors, built on free SAM.gov + USAspending data. Unanimous winner across three independent Veritas judges. Judges' consensus: $200–$900/mo by month 6, $400–$2,000/mo by month 12 (conservative-to-base), with one bounded constraint bend: ~2–6 hrs/week of founder QC on outgoing briefs.

## Method

27 research agents in three waves, plus synthesis:

1. **Evidence sweep** (13 agents) — verified free-tier limits (Bright Data, Nimble, Twilio, Google APIs, email, hosting, payments, marketplaces) and real market pricing/demand/precedents for candidate categories.
2. **Idea funnel** (5 agents) — independent ideation angles: tool-first, market-first, arbitrage-first, moat-first, precedent-first.
3. **Veritas council** (9 agents) — five adversarial lens critics (market, zero-budget reality, compliance, competition, operational autonomy) + a completeness critic + three independent judges.

All revenue figures are grounded in cited sources with as-of dates and confidence labels; the report is explicit about which numbers carry re-verify caveats.
