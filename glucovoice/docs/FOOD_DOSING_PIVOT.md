# Decision memo: food photo, CGM response and insulin dosing app

## 1. Verdict

Build the logging half as a free Android tool and do not build the dosing advice half until you hold FDA clearance. Every logging piece already ships free (Nightscout, xDrip+, Gluroo, Undermyfork, the Dexcom G7 app), while patient facing insulin dose advice from CGM data is a Class II medical device under product code QRX with no wellness or decision support exemption, and Apple bars uncleared dose calculators outright. Treat this as a personal tool and a learning path toward a partner or clinical route, not a business that pays in twelve months.

## 2. Biggest problems, ranked

1. **Dose advice is a regulated device.** FDA's January 29, 2026 decision support guidance says recommendations to patients "meet the definition of a device" and uses a carb plus glucose bolus calculator as its device example; the January 6, 2026 wellness policy excludes CGMs and "medication dosing" outputs (https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software). Solution: V1 shows zero dose numbers; pursue a 510(k) only with a funded partner.

2. **Apple blocks it independently.** Guideline 1.4.2 says dosage calculators must come from a manufacturer, hospital, insurer, pharmacy or hold FDA approval (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/). Solution: Android first; iOS after clearance.

3. **Your only real time glucose source is unofficial.** Dexcom's official API is retrospective, community Share clients can be switched off without notice, and the Nightscout Share bridge is already deprecated (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gagebenne/pydexcom/main/README.md, https://github.com/nightscout/nightscout-connect/issues/42). Solution: keep Share for yourself, apply to the Dexcom Developer Program now, and note V1 is retrospective so delayed data is fine.

4. **No sanctioned path to Omnipod 5 insulin history.** Tidepool's uploader rejects the Omnipod 5 controller, Android Health Connect has no insulin record type, and the only automated route is Glooko's undocumented API using the user's password, which Glooko's terms limit to personal, non commercial use (https://github.com/tidepool-org/uploader/blob/master/lib/core/device.js, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nightscout/nightscout-connect/main/README.md). Solution: manual bolus entry by default, Glooko CSV import as the upgrade, and an application to Glooko's partner portal (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/glooko/main/llms/glooko-llms.txt).

5. **Photo carb estimates are too rough to dose from.** Photo only carb error is about 40 to 57% MAPE, falling to about 20% only when the food's weight is known (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42502812/). Solution: the user confirms or edits every estimate before it saves, and the app remembers the corrected value per meal.

6. **Per food trends need many clean repeats.** Responses to one meal fall into two to three distinct clusters in T1D, and Autotune will not touch sensitivity with fewer than 10 data points (https://doi.org/10.2196/68821, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openaps/oref0/master/lib/autotune/index.js). Solution: show distributions, not averages; require about five uncontaminated repeats before a card appears (unverified estimate).

7. **The market pays nothing.** Nightscout, xDrip+, AndroidAPS, Sugarmate and Gluroo are free; Quin raised about £3.6m and ended as an asset sale; Bigfoot was absorbed by Abbott (https://github.com/nightscout/cgm-remote-monitor, https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/ayuda-heuristics). Solution: do not charge in year one; measure retention first.

## 3. What carries over from GlucoVoice

- **Kotlin Dexcom Share client.** Reuse it as one glucose source among several (Nightscout URL plus token, Glooko CSV, later the official Dexcom API). Never the only path.
- **Speech formatter.** Repurpose for voice meal entry and reading a per food card aloud. xDrip+ already offers voice treatment entry free (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NightscoutFoundation/xDrip/master/README.md).
- **Android shell.** Keep it. It sidesteps the Mac problem; Loop, Trio and iAPS build iOS through GitHub Actions but need a paid Apple account, and Apple's dosing rule applies regardless (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoopKit/loopdocs/main/docs/index.md).

## 4. Version 1 and version 2

**V1 (no clearance):**
- Meal log by photo, barcode, saved meal or manual entry; carbs, fat and protein confirmed by the user.
- Insulin entry: units, time relative to first bite, pump or pen; typed, or imported from Glooko CSV or Nightscout treatments.
- Automatic CGM overlay for four hours after each meal.
- Per food card: median and range of glucose rise, time to peak, share of repeats above 180 or below 70, doses actually logged, count of clean repeats.
- Confounder tags: exercise, illness, cycle phase, pod age, unlogged hypo treatment, suspected compression low overnight.
- PDF export for the clinician. In app disclaimer plus the Play Store medical device disclaimer.

**Bright line:** no suggested unit count, no "take more or less", no ratio or sensitivity change, no predicted glucose, no glucose alerts, no "manage your diabetes" marketing. The safe harbor is FDA enforcement discretion for self management software without treatment suggestions; the verifier found this is an extrapolation, not an FDA example, and the 2026 guidance treats a CGM stream as a "pattern" (https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/policy-device-software-functions-and-mobile-medical-applications). Get a consultant read before launch.

**V2 (clearance):** a CGM informed insulin dose calculator, product code QRX under 21 CFR 862.1358, Class II, 510(k), predicate Welldoc BlueStar K222888 (https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpcd/classification.cfm?id=QRX). Special controls require clinical validity data, a validated CGM interface (Share scraping will not pass), a user comprehension study, dosing error mitigation and secure transmission; every precedent is prescription or clinician authorized (https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-H/part-862/subpart-B/section-862.1358). Add 524B cybersecurity with an SBOM, IEC 62304 and ISO 13485.

## 5. Data plan by device

- **Omnipod 5, Android:** no API and no Health Connect insulin type; Glooko CSV import or credential sync with a 30 minute to overnight lag; manual by default. High confidence on the absence, medium on Glooko.
- **Omnipod 5, iPhone:** same Glooko path; no evidence the Omnipod app writes to Apple Health (unverified). Low.
- **Tandem:** only the undocumented Tandem Source API, which broke every user overnight on June 30 (https://github.com/jwoglom/tconnectsync/releases). Manual or CSV. High.
- **Medtronic:** unofficial CareLink clients need a browser login with reCAPTCHA and may breach terms (https://github.com/ondrej1024/carelink-python-client). Manual. High.
- **Smart pens:** iPhone HealthKit insulinDelivery carries a basal or bolus reason; Android reads NovoPen 6 by NFC as xDrip+ does and InPen by reverse engineered Bluetooth; Dexcom API v3 events return user logged insulin with delay (unverified) (https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/healthkit/hkquantitytypeidentifier/insulindelivery.json, https://github.com/NightscoutFoundation/xDrip/issues/2074). Medium.
- **Manual:** the first class default for everyone; Nightscout treatments import for DIY loopers. High.

## 6. Food photo pipeline

Design: the photo goes to a small vision model (Claude Haiku 4.5 class) that returns items, a portion guess, carbs, fat, protein and a confidence; the user confirms or edits with a portion slider; barcodes fall back to Open Food Facts (ODbL, 15 reads a minute, attribution required) and USDA FoodData Central (free, public domain; limits unverified) (https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/main/docs/api/index.md). Saved meals reuse the user's last corrected value rather than a fresh guess.

Expected error: photo only about 24 to 34 g on a 60 g meal, about 12 g with weight known; humans average 15 to 28 g error and miss by over 20 g in about 41% of meals (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23146371/, https://www.jmir.org/2016/5/e101/). The SNAQ trial showed user reviewed photo carbs raised time in range 6.6 points on a pump loop (https://www.thelancet.com/journals/eclinm/article/PIIS2589-5370(25)00470-5/fulltext).

Mitigations: mandatory confirm, plate or reference object prompt, one follow up question on hidden ingredients, a high fat and protein flag, and a range instead of a point.

Cost: about $0.003 to 0.005 per photo on Haiku 4.5, $0.006 to 0.01 on Sonnet 5, half in batch (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/vision). A thousand users at three photos a day is roughly $10 to 15 a day.

## 8. Regulatory path

Route: V1 needs no submission, but buy a consultant read and consider a 513(g) request (about $7,820, or $3,910 small business, unverified). V2 is a 510(k) under QRX. Fees from October 1, 2026 are about $28,653, or $7,163 with a small business determination, plus $13,785 annual registration (unverified). Consultant, QMS and cybersecurity documentation commonly run $60k to $150k, full programs around $250k (unverified, vendor sourced). Timeline: FDA's review goal is 90 days and UpDoc cleared in about 85 days with a direct predicate, but 862.1358 demands clinical validity data, so budget 12 to 24 months. Before spending: obtain the small business determination, hold a presubmission meeting to confirm QRX and the predicate, secure an official Dexcom data agreement, and recruit a clinician for the validity study. Clearance does not shield you from product liability (Riegel v. Medtronic). The EU route is Rule 11 class IIb with a notified body. The FTC Health Breach Notification Rule already applies to V1 (unverified).

## 9. Ninety day plan

Days 1 to 14: answer section 10; book one hour with a SaMD regulatory consultant; apply to the Dexcom Developer Program; write to Glooko's partner program and Tidepool; open a Google Play Organization account, required for health apps since January 2026 (unverified).
Days 15 to 45: build V1 on the GlucoVoice shell: meal log, insulin entry, CGM overlay, Glooko CSV import, per food card. Use it daily. Log your ten most common meals five times each.
Days 46 to 75: closed beta of 20 to 50 users from r/diabetes_t1 and the Nightscout and Gluroo communities. Ship weekly. Charge nothing.
Days 76 to 90: measure 30 day retention, share of users on manual versus imported insulin, and how many per food cards reach five clean repeats. Continue only if retention is strong and a clinician or regulatory partner has said yes.

Researcher summary: Paired meal, insulin and CGM data can validly support descriptive, per-food outcome memory (how glucose behaved after this food under the doses the user actually took, shown as a distribution with confounded repeats excluded), and that is roughly where every shipped system stops: Omnipod 5, Control-IQ and Loop all still require the user to announce carbs and initiate the meal bolus, and the only cleared "advisor" with an RCT (DreaMed Advisor Pro, non-inferior to physicians in 108 youths) is clinician-facing and adjusts settings every three weeks, not per meal.
