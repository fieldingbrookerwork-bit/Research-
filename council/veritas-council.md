# Veritas Council — Full Record

Convened 2026-08-26. Composition: five adversarial lens critics (market reality, zero-budget/tool reality, compliance & platform risk, competition & defensibility, operational autonomy) + one completeness critic, followed by three independent judges (expected-value, robustness, constraint-fit) ruling on the critiqued candidates. Verdict scale per candidate: kill / weaken / hold / strengthen.

## Critique — Market Reality & Pricing Evidence

### C1 — AI Visibility Watchdog — verdict: **WEAKEN**

- **(major)** *$99-$199/mo monitoring+interpretation retainer* — Verified: Otterly Lite is $29/mo with a GEO audit included on every tier; Merchynt sells actual GBP MANAGEMENT (work performed) at $99-$125/mo. A no-name report-only retainer is bracketed out: a cheaper tool below it, done-for-you at the same price above it. Clearing price for monitoring-only from an anonymous founder is $49-$99, and only if 'interpretation' is demonstrably valuable. ([source](https://otterly.ai/pricing))
- **(major)** *$149-$299 one-off audit price* — Comps (BizWhiz $249, ShE $297) are list prices with zero sales-volume evidence. The brief's only observed GEO transactions are Fiverr gigs at $10-$100 with THIN verified purchases, and funded competitors (AthenaHQ, Trakkr, Quno) give AI-visibility reports away free as lead magnets. C1 is charging $149-$299 for the industry's standard freebie. Realistic clearing price: $79-$149.
- **(major)** *10-20 positive replies -> 2-5 audit sales/mo* — Positive reply is not a sale. This is an admitted education-heavy sale to buyers with UNEVIDENCED willingness-to-pay, and the constraint bars human sales calls — every close must happen in AI-drafted email with no brand, no case studies, no precedent (brief: zero verified agent-run service businesses). Realistic close on positives: 5-15%, i.e., 0.5-3 audits/mo. Also 3.43% reply is a B2B benchmark; local-SMB front-desk inboxes reply worse.
- **(major)** *Month 6: 8-15 retainers = $800-$2,500 MRR* — Requires ~10-25 audits sold in 5 months, 40-60% retainer conversion, and near-zero churn. The brief's own facts cut against it: AI referral traffic is 0.32% of site traffic (ROI invisible to the client) and the adjacent AI-SDR category churns 50-70% in ~3 months. At 1-2 audits/mo, 40% conversion, 15-20% monthly churn: 2-4 retainers at month 6, not 8-15.
- **(major)** *500 Gmail sends/mo is a durable channel* — Gmail's 0.3% complaint threshold = one complaint per ~330 sends. Unsolicited 'ChatGPT recommends your competitor' emails to dental/legal/med-spa office inboxes plausibly exceed one complaint per month at 500 sends. The single revenue channel can zero out within 1-2 months, and there is no compliant $0 replacement per the constraint facts.
- **(minor)** *Local-SMB demand for GEO* — The brief itself concedes local-SMB willingness-to-pay for GEO is UNEVIDENCED and flags BrightLocal's 6%->45% consumer jump as possible framing inflation. The demand thesis rests on an upper-bound consumer-behavior stat, not one verified dollar of local-SMB GEO spend. The only verified GEO earners (AEO Engine $797-$2,997/mo) sell to a different, upmarket buyer.

**Adjusted view:** Survives only as a modest audit shop, not the modeled retainer machine. Corrected month 6: $150-$700/mo total (1-3 one-off audits at $79-$149 plus 0-4 retainers at $49-$99), contingent on Gmail deliverability surviving. Month 12: $300-$1,200/mo. The brief's $800-$2,500 month-6 MRR is a top-decile outcome presented as base case, inflated roughly 3-4x.

## Critique — COMPLIANCE & PLATFORM RISK

### C1 — AI Visibility Watchdog — verdict: **WEAKEN**

### Process verdict

The supply-side sweep is genuinely strong — the Twilio kill, the ESP cold-email ToS findings, the 'no verified precedent of human-only-collects-payment' result, and the two-sided GEO demand framing are exactly the adversarial work most plans skip. But three systematic failures should lower confidence in the shortlist itself. (1) The funnel-to-brief selection step is undocumented and lossy: the five ideation angles generated ~24 ideas; five became candidates and seven were dispositioned as rejects, but at least five others — including the market_first angle's TOP PICK (the GBP/review retainer, carrying the sweep's own 'deepest purchase evidence of any category examined') and the drift-correction retainer — vanished with no recorded rationale, and the survivors over-index on the GEO asset while the sweep's best marketplace-verified WTP data (review ops, GBP work, reinstatements) points elsewhere. (2) Verification status inflates between sweep and brief: 'VERIFIED' labels sit on numbers whose own sweep sections carry unresolved conflicts (Bright Data's Gmail gate and pool ambiguity), and the brief's funnel keeps the favorable vendor benchmarks while dropping the sweep's pessimistic counter-data (Smartlead 0.83% median; Belkins 0.016 meetings/100) and its bluntest conclusions ('the only precedented $0-viable shape is human-fronted sales'; 'the founder's real human job is not just collecting payment'; 'the monetizable step is implementation'). (3) All demand evidence is desk research — vendor surveys and sell-side price pages; zero buyer interviews or smoke tests were run even though the plan's own machinery makes them free, and the operational/legal substrate of the one human step (payment rails, tax, data-broker duties, the sales conversation itself) was never swept. Before committing agent-compute: write dispositions for the dropped six, verify asset states and the Bright Data pool on day 1, run two-week $0 pricing smoke tests for C1/C2, add a remediation SKU, and re-derive every month-12 range with churn, disclosure-discount, and refund terms. Until buyer-side evidence exists, treat the month-12 MRR figures as shapes, not forecasts.
