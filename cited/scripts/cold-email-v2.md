# Cited Labs — cold email, v2

Status: recommended primary. Replaces the v1 single-touch script by keeping its
evidence-first opener and adding the "why now" the founder asked for, plus the
follow-up sequence v1 never had.

## What changed from v1 and why

v1 (sent 2026-09-07 to 09-11, 25 prospects) opened with a verified error on the
prospect's own web presence. That part works and stays. Three things changed:

1. **Added the reason it matters now.** v1 said what was broken but never said
   why this year is different from last year. That is the gap the founder
   identified.
2. **Added touches 2, 3 and 4.** v1 was one touch. Most cold replies arrive on
   touch 2-4. Going 1 touch -> 4 touches is a larger lift than any wording change.
3. **The proof moved into the email.** v1 asked for a reply to receive the
   finding. v2 shows one query result up front and holds the rest back.

## The claim we do NOT make

"Search engines are dying / falling out of usage" is not true and is not in this
script. Google runs about 5 trillion searches a year and roughly 80% of global
query volume, and total volume is still growing. Any owner with an SEO vendor
knows this and will discount everything after that sentence.

What IS true, and what this script uses instead:

- ChatGPT is now the #2 place people ask questions, at roughly 12% of Google's
  query volume, having passed Bing.
- Google searches per US user fell about 20% year over year, because AI
  summaries answer the question in place.
- The shift is concentrated in informational queries. "Who should I call for a
  water heater" is exactly that kind of query.
- **Assistants send almost no referral traffic.** When an assistant names a
  competitor instead of you, nothing appears in your analytics. The loss is
  invisible. This is the strongest honest line available and it is the hook.

## Rules that do not bend

- Every number and quote in touch 1 must have been verified on the prospect's
  own pages or listings before sending. A wrong "your site says X" kills the
  entire pitch in one line.
- Real physical address and real opt-out in every touch. Honor removals same day.
- No links in touches 1 and 2 on a cold domain. Links can appear from touch 3.
- Never claim a ranking factor or how an assistant decides. Say what was observed.
- Stop the whole sequence on any reply, including "no thanks".
