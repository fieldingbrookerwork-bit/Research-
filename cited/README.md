## Posture, in one place

- The free check is the demo. Hand it over with no call attached, within 24
  hours of them asking. Offer it on every surface, in every pitch.
- Open scope: automation, follow-up, reviews, CRM, websites, ads - whatever gets
  a client. Say so in every pitch. Answer completely and for free first, then
  make the offer; never lead with it.
- Every reply gets a response the same day, including the rejections.
- Specific and verified beats general and true. Every claim about a prospect's
  own site must have been read on that site first.
- Nothing is sent or posted from an agent session. Drafts only; the founder
  sends and posts.
